Presenter mode. Host is Kaif. Scene 1 attaches `__reference/kaif_tech_news_set.jpg`. That photo is the face and the room. Do not swap in the peach-wall portrait.

Do not say every AI is dangerous. Do not call for a ban. Do not add the July Hugging Face incident. Do not say a monitor was removed and then denied, and do not say there was no harm outside the company, because this script does not use those details.
