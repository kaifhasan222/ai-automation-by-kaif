# Demo short — Kiraya

Pehla episode. Sirf script aur Flow prompts. Video abhi generate nahi karna.

Style: 9:16, photoreal cinematic, Hindi dialogue. Har clip 8 second. Chhe clips = 48 second. Music baad mein edit mein. Clips ke andar background score nahi.

Revenge khoon se nahi. Seth ne maa ka deposit rok kar Arjun ka college form mitti mein phenka. Arjun usi ki awaaz colony ke speaker pe chala deta hai.

Flow ne 17 saal ke minor character ko policy pe rok diya. Demo character ab 22 saal ka adult hai. Prompt mein "boy" ya school-child mat likhna.

## Assumptions

- Ek hi short. Series nahi.
- Dialogue lock hai. Lambi line ko clip ke beech mat kaatna, neeche wali lines 8 second mein fit hoti hain.
- Characters fictional hain. Koi real photo attach nahi karni.
- Flow mein pehle character aur location images banao, phir video. Naya angle ho to last frame mat lagana. Wahi angle jaari ho to pichhle clip ka last frame starting image banao.

## Logline

Raat ko chai ki dukaan pe form dho raha Arjun sun leta hai ki seth ne uski maa ka deposit rakh liya hai. Subah woh recording chala deta hai.

## Hook

Do option the. Yeh wala use karna hai, word-to-word:

> जिस आदमी की माँ का किराया रोका गया, उसे क्या पता था कि रात को सच्चाई खुद बोल पड़ेगी?

Narrator alag awaaz. Arjun is line ko muh se nahi bolega.

## Characters

| Naam | Kaun | Look lock |
|---|---|---|
| Arjun | About 30, adult | Thin, moustache, short black hair, faded white shirt, dark trousers, rubber slippers, one torn rent receipt |
| Savitri | Maa, about 40 | Pale green cotton saree, hair in a low bun, thin gold studs, tired eyes |
| Seth Vinod | Landlord, about 55 | Cream kurta, gold ring on right hand, neat moustache, heavy body |

Arjun ki awaaz: low, slow, almost no emotion. Hindi, city accent.
Savitri: soft, mid pitch, short breath before she speaks.
Seth: heavy, confident, slightly nasal. Phone pe dheema, speaker pe wahi awaaz.
Narrator: calm storyteller. Sirf Scene 1.

## Locations

1. Night lane outside a small tea stall. One bulb, wet road, steel glasses, wooden counter. No crowd noise.
2. Morning colony courtyard. Yellow building, one speaker on a pole, clothesline, same doors in every angle.
3. Night kitchen-and-room. One doorway. Seth inside on a plastic chair. Arjun in the dark kitchen. Same bulb, warm and dim.

## Shot plan

| Clip | Time | Method | Shot | Kaun bolta hai |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | New image | Medium, night stall | Sirf narrator |
| 2 | 0:08–0:16 | New angle, flashback | Courtyard, form in mud | Seth |
| 3 | 0:16–0:24 | New angle | Doorway, phone recording | Seth, phone pe |
| 4 | 0:24–0:32 | New angle | Speaker pole | Recording start |
| 5 | 0:32–0:40 | New angle | Seth close, then Arjun | Recording end. Dono chup |
| 6 | 0:40–0:48 | Last frame se continue | Maa aur Arjun | Arjun ek line |

## Scene 1 — Hook

0:00–0:08. Raat. Arjun akela stall pe. Form shirt ke pocket mein phata hua dikhe.

Attach: Arjun ki clean portrait image. Location: night tea stall.

Camera: medium shot, counter tak. Dheema push-in uske haath tak. Koi doosra move nahi.

```
IMAGE. 9:16. Photoreal. A 22-year-old Indian man, Arjun, with a light beard, behind a tiny night tea stall. Thin face, short black hair, faded white shirt, dark trousers, rubber slippers. He holds a wet steel glass with both hands. A torn college form sticks from his shirt pocket. One warm bulb, wet lane, wooden counter, no crowd. Neutral tired expression. Plain cinematic still, not a contact sheet.
```

```
VIDEO. 8 seconds. 9:16. Photoreal. Start on that exact still. Arjun does not speak and does not lip-sync. He slowly sets the glass down and touches the torn form in his pocket. One slow push-in from his waist to his hands. Quiet night ambience only, no music, no other voices. Narrator, calm Hindi, about 0:01 to 0:07: "जिस नौजवान का फॉर्म भीड़ में मिट्टी में फेंका गया, उसे क्या पता था कि रात को सच्चाई खुद बोल पड़ेगी?" End with his fingers on the form, eyes down. This frame can start a continued shot, but Scene 2 is a new daytime angle, so do not use it as Scene 2's start.
```

## Scene 2 — Receipt

0:08–0:16. Subah ka courtyard. Seth kiraye ki receipt mitti mein phenkta hai. Arjun chup.

Attach: Arjun portrait, Seth portrait, courtyard master. Scene 1 ka last frame mat lagana. Yeh naya din hai.

Camera: medium wide. Seth right hand se receipt neeche. Arjun left side pe. 180 line mat badalna.

```
IMAGE. 9:16. Same adult Arjun, moustache, faded white shirt, now in a morning colony courtyard. Seth Vinod: cream kurta, gold ring on the right hand, neat moustache, about 55. Seth has just let a torn rent receipt fall toward wet mud. Arjun stands left, jaw tight, eyes on the paper, mouth closed. Yellow building and one speaker pole behind them. Natural morning light.
```

```
VIDEO. 8 seconds. 9:16. Start on that image. No narrator. Seth speaks once, heavy confident Hindi, finished before the last second: "यहाँ सिर्फ किराया चलता है। रसीद मत माँगना।" He flicks the rent receipt into the mud with his right hand and turns away. Arjun, a man in his thirties, does not speak. His eyes stay on the paper. One small inhale. No second camera move. End: receipt in mud, Arjun still, Seth's back half-turned.
```

## Scene 3 — Recording

0:16–0:24. Raat. Kitchen andheri. Door ke us paar Seth phone pe.

Attach: Seth portrait, kitchen-room master. Arjun sirf side se, chehra kam. Naya angle.

```
IMAGE. 9:16. Night. A narrow open doorway. Inside, Seth Vinod sits on a plastic chair in a cream kurta, phone at his right ear, gold ring visible. Outside the door, in a dark kitchen, Arjun holds his own cheap phone low, screen facing Seth, recording. Arjun's mouth is closed. One warm bulb. No other person.
```

```
VIDEO. 8 seconds. 9:16. Start on that image. Arjun stays silent, no lip movement. Seth, unaware, speaks into the phone in the same heavy voice: "डिपाजिट मैंने रख लिया। रसीद मत देना।" Quiet room tone. No music. The phone in Arjun's hand stays pointed through the door. End: recording still running, Seth's mouth just closed, Arjun still hidden. Do not continue this frame into the courtyard. Next scene is the next morning.
```

## Scene 4 — Speaker

0:24–0:32. Agli subah. Wahi courtyard. Arjun phone ko pole ke speaker ke paas rakhta hai.

Attach: Arjun portrait, courtyard master. Scene 3 ka frame mat lagana.

```
IMAGE. 9:16. Morning. Same courtyard and the same speaker pole from Scene 2. Arjun, same clothes, stands alone under the pole and holds his phone near the microphone. Mouth closed. A few neighbours are only soft shapes in the background, not main faces. Seth is not in this frame yet.
```

```
VIDEO. 8 seconds. 9:16. Arjun does not speak. He presses play. From the speaker, Seth's same recorded voice begins, slightly filtered: "डिपाजिट मैंने रख लिया।" Arjun's eyes stay forward. One steady shot, no zoom. End: his thumb still on the phone, the speaker the loudest thing in the courtyard.
```

## Scene 5 — Reaction

0:32–0:40. Seth sunta hai. Arjun nazar nahi hatata.

Attach: dono portraits, courtyard. Scene 4 ka last frame continuity ke liye, lekin camera naya hai, isliye nayi starting image.

```
IMAGE. 9:16. Same morning courtyard, same moment. Close view. Seth Vinod in the cream kurta has just heard his own voice. His smile is gone, right hand half-lifted, gold ring visible. Behind him, soft and out of focus, Arjun stands by the speaker pole looking straight at him, mouth closed. No new props.
```

```
VIDEO. 8 seconds. 9:16. Nobody speaks live. The speaker finishes the same line, then stops: "रसीद मत देना।" Seth's eyes move to Arjun. His hand freezes. Arjun does not blink away and does not smile. Hold the look. No music. End on Arjun's steady face, usable only as emotional state. Scene 6 needs a new framing with the mother.
```

## Scene 6 — Kal raseed

0:40–0:48. Maa aur Arjun. Receipt stool pe.

Attach: Arjun, Savitri, courtyard. Scene 5 ka emotional reference, starting frame nahi.

```
IMAGE. 9:16. Same courtyard, minutes later. Savitri in a pale green saree, hair in a low bun, stands beside adult Arjun. The torn rent receipt lies on a small wooden stool between them. Seth is a blurred figure behind. Mother's hand rests on Arjun's shoulder.
```

```
VIDEO. 8 seconds. 9:16. Arjun speaks once, low and slow: "कल रसीद ले लूँगा।" Savitri nods once, eyes wet, and does not speak. Gentle pull-back. No music. End with the two of them and the receipt visible.
```

## Lock dialogue

Narrator, Scene 1:

> जिस आदमी की माँ का किराया रोका गया, उसे क्या पता था कि रात को सच्चाई खुद बोल पड़ेगी?

Seth, Scene 2:

> यहाँ सिर्फ किराया चलता है। रसीद मत माँगना।

Seth on phone, Scene 3, then speaker in Scene 4–5:

> डिपाजिट मैंने रख लिया। रसीद मत देना।

Arjun, Scene 6:

> कल रसीद ले लूँगा।

## Continuity note

Arjun abhi bhi wahi faded white shirt pehene hai. Form phata hua hai par mitti saaf hai, stool pe pada hai. Deposit abhi haath mein nahi aaya. Seth peechhe blur hai, log usse munh mod chuke hain. Agli scene agar bani to school gate ka naya location chahiye. Is short ka end yahin hai.

## Flow pe kaise chalana hai

1. Pehle upar ki IMAGE prompts se Arjun, Savitri, Seth, stall, aur courtyard banao.
2. Jo image theek lage usi ko lock samjho. Doosri mat banao jab tak chehra na badle.
3. VIDEO prompts ek-ek karke. Scene 2, 3, 4, 6 nayi starting image. Scene 5 naya angle hai, last frame se camera mat moro.
4. Chhe mp4 `__reference/kiraya_clips/` mein daal dena. Main unhe jod dunga.
